## 🚫 Do NOT do this
- Don't **buy backlinks** or use "1000 backlinks for $5" services — Google penalises this.
- Don't spam the same comment/link on random blogs.
- Don't create fake reviews or fake directory listings.
Quality beats quantity every time. 20 real local listings > 1000 junk links.

## 🔁 Already done for you (on-site SEO — the other half)
- Strong **internal linking** (every suburb + service page cross-links)

add the site link to your Google Business Profile. That combo does more than any paid
"backlink package" ever will.
