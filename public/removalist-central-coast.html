Questions

Removalist Central Coast, FAQs

How much does a removalist cost on the Central Coast?
Every move is priced on its size and distance, so you get a fixed, upfront price before we start, with no hidden costs.
Do you do single-item removals?
Yes. A lounge, a fridge, a mattress or one bulky item is no problem, we do single-item removals every day and you only pay for what you move.
Can you move a whole house or apartment?
We handle small-to-medium home and apartment moves across the Central Coast. For large multi-room moves we'll tell you honestly if a bigger truck is the better call.
Do you pick up Marketplace and Gumtree buys?
Absolutely. We collect your Facebook Marketplace or Gumtree purchase from the seller and deliver it to your door, so you don't need to borrow a ute or a trailer.
Which areas do you cover?
The whole Central Coast, from Gosford and the Woy Woy Peninsula to Wyong, The Entrance and Toukley, plus up to Newcastle and the Hunter.
Do you help with the heavy lifting?
Always. We do all the lifting, carrying and loading, and we treat your gear like our own. You just point to where it goes.
