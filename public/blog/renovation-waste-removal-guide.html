Renovation Waste Removal: Keeping Your Site Safe

A reno makes mess faster than progress. Here's how to keep building waste under control so the job stays safe and on schedule.

By Matt from Lanky Services · 2026-08-18

Get a free quote Call 0439 973 051

A tidy site is a safe site

Offcuts, plasterboard, tiles and rubble are trip hazards and slow the trades down. Regular clean-ups keep everyone safe and the job moving.

Clear between trades

Booking a debris clear-out between stages, after demo, after fit-out, keeps the site workable and stops mess piling up into one giant job at the end.

What we take

Demolition debris, timber, plasterboard, tiles, bricks and general builders rubble. We turn it around fast so you are never waiting on a skip.

Do your homework

Helpful resources & sources

Want to check the facts or drop something off yourself? These are the official NSW and Central Coast waste authorities we work alongside:

  • Central Coast Council – Waste & Recycling — bin days, tip locations and what goes where.
  • NSW EPA – Recycling & Reuse — the state rules on waste, recycling and safe disposal.
  • Planet Ark – Recycling Near You — find a drop-off point for almost anything.
  • NSW EPA – Illegal Dumping — why dumping rubbish is illegal (and what it costs).
