How Much Does Rubbish Removal Cost on the Central Coast?

The honest answer to the most-asked question in rubbish removal: it depends on how much you've got and what it is. Here's how the pricing actually works on the Central Coast.

By Matt from Lanky Services · 2026-08-18

Get a free quote Call 0439 973 051

Rubbish removal cost: priced by volume, not weight

Good rubbish removal on the Central Coast is priced by how much room your load takes up in the van, not by the kilo. A couple of bags is cheap; a full ute-load of hard rubbish costs more. Heavy or light, if it fills the same space it is the same price, so a load of bricks costs the same as a load of pillows.

What affects the price

The amount, the type of waste (general junk versus heavy building rubble), how easy it is to get to, and whether it needs sorting all move the price. Tip fees are built in, so there are no nasty surprises at the end. Awkward access, like up a flight of stairs, can add a little.

How to get a cheap, accurate quote

The fastest way to get a real price is to send us a photo of the pile. You will get an upfront, no-obligation quote before we lift a thing. Book a few jobs together or clear a full load and the per-item cost drops.
