Hoarding Clean-Ups: How to Approach It With Care

Hoarding clean-ups need patience and zero judgement. Here's how to approach one gently, whether it's your home or a loved one's.

By Matt from Lanky Services · 2026-08-18

Get a free quote Call 0439 973 051

Go gently, and get consent

A hoarding clean-up works best when the person is on board and in control. Push too hard and it backfires. Small wins, one area at a time, build trust and momentum.

Sort with respect

Set aside anything genuinely important, work in sections, and celebrate progress. It is emotional work, so take breaks and be kind about it.

Discreet, judgement-free removal

We handle heavy or large-scale clean-ups quietly and without judgement, at whatever pace feels right. No lectures, just a hand getting it sorted.

Do your homework

Helpful resources & sources

Want to check the facts or drop something off yourself? These are the official NSW and Central Coast waste authorities we work alongside:

  • Central Coast Council – Waste & Recycling — bin days, tip locations and what goes where.
  • NSW EPA – Recycling & Reuse — the state rules on waste, recycling and safe disposal.
  • Planet Ark – Recycling Near You — find a drop-off point for almost anything.
  • NSW EPA – Illegal Dumping — why dumping rubbish is illegal (and what it costs).
