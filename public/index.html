Questions, answered

Central Coast rubbish removal FAQs

The stuff people actually ask before they book, pricing, same-day, what we take, the lot. Can't see yours? Give us a bell.

How much does Central Coast rubbish removal cost?
Every job's different, so we price by how much room your rubbish takes up in the van and what it is, not by the kilo. Send us a quick photo of the pile and you'll get an upfront, no-surprises quote before we lift a thing. (The pile, sadly, still won't move itself.)
Do you charge by weight or by volume?
By volume, how much space it takes in the van or trailer. Heavy or light, if it fits the same spot, it's the same price. Nice and simple.
Do you offer same-day rubbish removal?
Most days, yep. Same-day and next-day bookings are usually available right across the Central Coast and Newcastle. Give us a bell and we'll tell you the soonest we can get there.
What rubbish do you take away?
Household junk, old furniture, mattresses, fridges and whitegoods, green and garden waste, hard rubbish, garage and shed clean-outs, e-waste and renovation debris. If it fits in the van, chances are it's gone.
Is there anything you can't take?
A few things need a licensed specialist, asbestos, chemicals and other hazardous stuff. If you're not sure, flick us a photo and we'll tell you straight.
Do I need to be home for the pick-up?
Not always. As long as we can safely get to the rubbish and you've okayed the quote, we can sort it while you're at work or the beach. Your call.
Do you do the heavy lifting, and recycle it?
Yep to both. Our fully insured crew does all the lifting, loading and sweep-up, backyard, garage, up the stairs, wherever it's hiding. Then we donate what's still good and recycle what we can so less ends up in landfill.
Can you grab something I bought online?
Too right. We're also a local pickup and delivery service, snagged a bargain on Facebook Marketplace or Gumtree that won't fit the car? We'll collect it and drop it to your door.
