How pricing works

How much does rubbish removal cost on the Central Coast?

Straight answer: it depends on how much you've got and what it is. We price by volume, how much room your rubbish takes up in the truck, not by the kilo. Physics does the pricing, not your bathroom scales. Heavy or light, if it fills the same space, it's the same price.

Priced by volume

We quote on how much space the load takes in the truck. A few bags is cheap; a full ute-load of hard rubbish costs more. Simple and fair.

What affects the price

The amount, the type (general junk vs heavy building waste), access to the pile, and whether it needs sorting. Tip fees for the Central Coast are built in.

The best way to get a price

Send us a quick photo of the pile. You'll get an upfront, no-obligation quote before we lift a thing, no surprises on the day.

As a rough industry guide across NSW, general rubbish is often quoted per cubic metre and single items like a mattress or fridge have a set price. Rather than guess, we give you a real quote for your job. Prices vary, so send a photo for an exact price.
